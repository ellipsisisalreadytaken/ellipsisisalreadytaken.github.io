# Home page (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a founder, consultant or agency owner who heard about Ellipsa. Action: Windows visitors download the beta; Mac visitors join the Mac waitlist. Proof: the mechanism shown working (people, promises, the day), privacy facts, connected apps. No testimonials, counts or prices exist; demo people are illustrative and labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every person you work with has a pigeonhole; Ellipsa sorts your day into them and whatever sticks out is what you owe. Refuses the category default of a dark gradient hero with a floating app screenshot and three feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: A post room after hours. Deep enamel-navy steel rack (#16264F) on a near-black navy room (#0E1426), one warm lamp. Paper slips in cool white (#E9ECEF) with coloured edges: amber (#F2B544) a promise you made, signal red (#E2553F) overdue, white waiting on them. Brass label frames carry names in expanded grotesk caps (Archivo, wide axis); body in Archivo normal width. Stamped dates are the only monospace. Controls are brass label plates and paper slips; corners square-ish (4px), no pills except the waitlist input row.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor sees the rack, the headline and Download for Windows. Scrolling walks one day along the rack (8:30 briefing, 1:55 before a call, 2:20 during it, 4:10 after) with the camera moving to the slip each moment is about. Then the sorting log of what Ellipsa does, the privacy facts in plain words, connected apps, and a close with Download plus the Mac waitlist.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed three.js rack filling the right two-thirds, lit from the top left, slips protruding from named cells. Left column: headline at about 4.5rem, 20-word subtext, brass Download for Windows button and a quiet Mac waitlist link. Bottom right: the slip reverse panel with brass name tabs to pick a slip.

FORM: The Post Room, position 6 on the grounded list, seed key 38606843 (re-roll 1). Signature interaction: hover or tab to a slip and it slides out of its pigeonhole while its dense reverse (source, date, promise) shows in the panel. Raises: one rack owns a quiet field (from the catalog sleeve); every slip has a denser reverse (from the insert card).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
