# Home page (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a founder, consultant or agency owner who heard about Ellipsa. Action: Windows visitors download the beta; Mac visitors join the Mac waitlist. Proof: the real app (a screen recording and screenshots taken with demo data), the mechanism shown, privacy facts, connected apps. No testimonials, counts or prices; demo people are illustrative and labelled.

Pinned by the founder (8 Oct 2026): use Ellipsa's own colours and identity, a bouncy animated feel, and a mascot that plays on the ellipsis. "Go wild." The earlier Post Room direction was rejected as not Ellipsa.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The ellipsis is the mascot. Three dots that bounce like a typing indicator when Ellipsa is thinking, gather into a face when it talks to you, turn red when it listens, and finish the sentences you left at "…". Refuses both the dark AI-gradient hero and the earlier skeuomorphic post room.

OWN-WORLD: The app's own monochrome: white ground, ink #030213, greys #F3F3F5 / #ECECF0 / #717182, the grey pebble button (#E5E7EB) with three ink dots. Red #EF4444 only for listening, blue #2563EB only for the pending-actions badge. Lowercase italic Crimson Text for the wordmark and display lines; Inter for everything else. Soft round shapes (the pebble, 16 to 24px radii, pill buttons like the app), springs everywhere (stiffness about 260, damping about 20, like the app's button).

STORY: The hero sentence ends in a live ellipsis, "you said you'd get back to them…", and the dots react to you. Scrolling, the dots hop down into the pebble that sits bottom-right like the real button and play each moment of the day in character (list, face, red listening pulse, typing, badge). Then the real app on screen, what it does, privacy, connected apps, download and the Mac waitlist, where the dots celebrate.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White. Left: the italic line at about 5rem with three mascot dots as its last characters, a one-sentence Inter subtext, a black pill Download for Windows and a quiet Mac waitlist link. Right: the big grey pebble (the mascot's home) at about 340px with the dots alive in it, looking at the cursor.

FORM: Founder-pinned direction (no roll); based on the app's FloatingButton, globals.css and ChatOverlay wordmark. Signature interaction: the dots follow the cursor as eyes, squash when clicked, and travel with the scroll from the headline into the pebble. Motion grammar: springs only, squash and stretch on landing, staggered 3-beat rhythm (the typing indicator).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
