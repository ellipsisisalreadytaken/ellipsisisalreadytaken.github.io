it remembers. only for you.

  • On your computer. Your memory is stored on your own computer, encrypted, with daily encrypted backups.
  • Your yes first. Nothing is sent, booked, changed or deleted until you approve it.
  • No bot in your calls. Nobody joins the meeting, and call audio is never saved to disk.

To answer you, Ellipsa sends only what a question needs to OpenAI, through Ellipsa’s relay. The privacy policy sets out exactly what goes where.
