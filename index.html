Everything lands in the right slot.

  1. Day one

    Reads the last 30 days of Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion and GitHub, so your first briefing already knows your people.

  2. Every morning

    A briefing of who is waiting on you, what you promised and who you're meeting. It can read it aloud.

  3. Each person

    A card with how you talk, what each side promised, and whether the relationship is going quiet.

  4. In calls

    Answers from your memory while you talk, and the action items waiting when you hang up.

  5. Replies

    Drafts in your voice and calls booked in your free slots. Anything only you can fill in is marked, and sending waits until it's done.

  6. Other AI apps

    The same memory inside Claude, Cursor, VS Code and five more, through MCP.

Your memory stays on your computer.

Encrypted on disk. Your memory is stored on your own computer, encrypted, with daily encrypted backups.

You approve every action. Nothing is sent, booked, changed or deleted until you say yes.

Calls stay yours. No bot joins the meeting, and call audio is never saved to disk.

To answer you, Ellipsa sends only what a question needs to OpenAI, through Ellipsa's relay. The privacy policy sets out exactly what goes where.

Works with what you already use.

Several Google accounts at once. Your memory also works in Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, Gemini CLI, Codex and LM Studio.
