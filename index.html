Ellipsa

A personal desktop assistant that remembers your context and works alongside you.

Ellipsa runs on your own computer. It keeps a private memory of the people, conversations and tasks you work with, gives you a morning briefing, and helps you act on them: drafting email replies, scheduling meetings and reminding you about things you meant to follow up on.

When you connect your Google account, Ellipsa can read and organise your Gmail, prepare drafts for your review, send email you approve, and manage events on your Google Calendar. Actions that send, delete or change things are held for your approval first.

Ellipsa is a personal project in private testing and is not offered to the public.
