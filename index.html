Ellipsa

A desktop assistant that remembers the people and work in your day, and helps you follow up.

Ellipsa runs on your own Windows computer. It keeps a private memory of the people you work with, your conversations, meetings and tasks, and uses it to give you a daily briefing, point out things you meant to follow up on, answer questions about your work, draft emails in your own voice and schedule calls.

Connected services

You choose what to connect, and you can disconnect at any time:

  • Google (Gmail and Calendar): your email and schedule, drafts for your review, and meetings you set up.
  • Slack: the conversations you are part of.
  • Notion: only the pages you choose to share with Ellipsa.
  • GitHub: issues and pull requests you are involved in.

Anything that sends, deletes or changes something, such as sending an email or creating a calendar event, waits for your approval first.

Your memory is stored on your computer. How your information is handled is set out in the Privacy Policy.

Ellipsa is pre-release software in private testing with invited users. It is not offered to the general public.
