Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Ellipsa is a desktop assistant that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what information it uses, where that information is kept, and the choices you have.

Information Ellipsa uses

  • Google account information. Your name, email address and profile picture, used to identify your account inside the app.
  • Gmail. Message contents and metadata, used to summarise your inbox, find emails that need a reply, prepare draft replies, send email you approve, and apply labels.
  • Google Calendar. Your events, used to show your schedule in your briefing and to create or update events you approve.
  • Activity you choose to share. Depending on your settings: the active window title, screenshots, clipboard text and meeting audio you record. Each of these can be turned off in Settings, and specific apps and websites can be excluded.

Where information is stored

Ellipsa stores its memory (events, people, tasks and drafts) in databases on your own computer. Your Google sign-in tokens are also stored on your computer. Ellipsa does not run a server that collects your data.

Processing by AI services

To summarise, extract tasks and draft replies, Ellipsa sends the relevant text (for example, an email you received or a meeting transcript) to OpenAI's API for processing. Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train their models. Nothing is sent to any other third party.

Use of Google user data

Ellipsa's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the features you see in Ellipsa.
  • It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.
  • It is not transferred to anyone, except to the AI processing service described above as needed to provide those features, or if required by law.
  • No person reads your data. The only exception is if you explicitly ask for help with a problem and give permission.
  • Google user data is not used to train general AI or machine-learning models.

Retention and deletion

Ellipsa keeps information according to the retention periods you choose in Settings. You can delete all stored data at any time with Settings → Delete all data. You can revoke Ellipsa's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops further access but does not delete data already stored on your computer.

Children

Ellipsa is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
